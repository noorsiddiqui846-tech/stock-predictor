🎲 How does the app work this out?
  1. Nobody can know the future, so the computer imagines it, 1,000 times.
  2. Each imagined future is built from the last 30 days of : for every new day, it picks one past day at random and repeats its move (like "up 1.2%" or "down 0.8%").
  3. Then we count. If 580 of the 1,000 futures went up, we say "58% chance up".
  4. The likely range is where the middle 8 in 10 futures ended. So the real price should land outside it about 2 times in 10. That's normal, not a mistake.

⚠️ The futures copy the past. If the last 30 days were unusual, the prediction will be too.
